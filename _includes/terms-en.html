Class 1
Blocking. The result, or a central part of it, cannot be used. Example: the app crashes at launch, or the login doesn't work.
Class 2
Serious. An important function is clearly restricted and there is no reasonable workaround. Example: payments work, but receipts are never sent.
Class 3
Minor. Everything else. It doesn't block acceptance and gets fixed afterwards. Example: a button is 2 pixels out of place, or there is a typo in the settings screen.
